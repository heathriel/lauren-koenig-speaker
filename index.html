About Lauren

From C-Suite to Center Stage

Lauren Koenig doesn't just talk about the future—she's building it. As Co-Founder and Chief Marketing Officer of CELITECH, she pioneered AI-enabled eSIM technology that transformed how travelers stay connected globally.

Her journey from the American Midwest to New York City, with stops in France and Silicon Valley, shaped her unique perspective on innovation. Before CELITECH, she founded TWIP (Travel With Interesting People), where she developed the patent-pending Travanality AI-driven personalization framework—technology that established new standards in how brands engage with consumers.

Lauren's work has earned recognition from global innovators, including the Lufthansa Innovation Award and Phocuswright People's Choice Award. Her ability to bridge cutting-edge AI with practical leadership makes her the definitive voice at the intersection of technology and human potential.

The Travanality Story

What happens when you combine deep travel expertise with cutting-edge AI? You get personalization technology that doesn't just predict behavior—it understands people.

The Travanality framework grew from Lauren's work at TWIP, where she saw a critical gap: travel brands were collecting data on what people booked, but not on why they traveled. Her patent-pending AI technology empowers brands to engage with consumers at a meaningful, highly customized level.

This isn't theoretical innovation. This is technology that earned the Lufthansa Innovation Award and Phocuswright People's Choice Award— recognition from the travel industry's most discerning audiences.
